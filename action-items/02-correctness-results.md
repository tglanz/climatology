# 02 Correctness implementation results

Status: complete on branch `correctness`, with the verification limits below.
[Plan](02-correctness-plan.md) and [progress](02-correctness-plan-progress.md).

## Result

- Gaussian geometry and quadrature have one owner. SFNO transforms and coordinate
  features use actual Isca coordinates with explicit latitude orientation.
- Raw preparation and evaluation share sample construction, detectors, targets,
  unit checks, and rejection reasons. Every target channel is retained.
- Prepared data retain per-simulation targets and input-unit identity.
  Empty training data and cross-split replicas fail explicitly.
- Stable population moments, persisted epsilon, and one normalization API give
  matching training and checkpoint arithmetic. Losses average by sample count and
  stop before nonfinite losses or gradients update weights.
- Baselines use training simulations only. Physical scores and figures remain
  separate by target. Undefined relative errors are counted. Reports include only
  artifacts generated by the current evaluation.
- Spinup is described as an adjacent-block heuristic. Convergence means agreement
  with a finite-run reference. Replicate deviation is descriptive, without a
  claimed irreducible-error bound. See [methods](../docs/pre-processing.md),
  [norms](../docs/norms.md), and [grids](../docs/grids.md).

## Real-data experiment

Artifacts are local and untracked:
[configuration](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/config.toml),
[runner](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/run.py),
[measurements](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/summary.json),
[sample verification](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/sample-verification.json),
[final-source verification](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/final-source-verification.json), and
[evaluation report](../output/correctness-20261111-final-z1q9u8_d/training/evaluation/report.md).
The measurements retain ordered raw-file content hashes and source-code hashes.

Used one replica from each of three existing T85 configurations, 150 segments
and 1050 snapshots each. Each split contains
`x=(2,10,128,256)` and `y=(2,2,128)`.
Targets are zonal wind and relative vorticity. Windows have length 10, stride 11,
and limit 2. Spinup uses threshold 2, block size 10, and minimum stability 3.
Convergence uses threshold 0.05 and hold 3.

The model is a one-layer SFNO with four hidden channels and mode counts (4,8).
Training used CPU, seed 17, two epochs, batch size 3, the Gaussian-weighted relative
objective, Adam, learning rate 0.001, and gradient clipping at 1.

| Split | Simulation | Spinup index | Convergence endpoint | Window starts | Target interval |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| train | 10bb40-0 | 10 | 282 | [10, 21] | [282, 1050] |
| val | 0f0ad6-0 | 20 | 246 | [20, 31] | [246, 1050] |
| test | 021787-0 | 10 | 755 | [10, 21] | [755, 1050] |

All real input windows, targets, and per-simulation target records match prepared
arrays exactly. Reloaded predictions with the same batch partition match exactly.
Changing the prediction batch partition gives maximum absolute difference
4.76837158e-07. Validation scores with batch sizes one and two are
1.11439055204 and 1.11439061165.
Both comparisons satisfy the declared absolute and relative tolerance of 2e-6.

Preparation took 4.910 seconds; training took 0.558 seconds;
evaluation and provenance recording took 4.621 seconds.
The complete runner took 10.175 seconds and peaked at 1767.5 MiB
resident memory. Later sample/source verification is additional.

The two held-out simulation relative errors are 1.081428 for wind and 1.174029
for vorticity. The zero predictor scores one. This deliberately tiny run validates
integration and does not demonstrate model quality improvement. It has one replica
per configuration, so it does not estimate replicate variability.

The retained parameter metadata are absent for this raw experiment; the nearest
configuration baseline is explicitly unavailable. Synthetic regressions cover
nearest predictions, parameter scaling, test-target isolation, and missing metadata.

## Independent review

Correctness, tests, and structure reviewers inspected the local diff. An adversarial
pass challenged findings and checked the fixes. All accepted findings are resolved:

- Separate the unweighted objective from Gaussian weighting.
- Accept decoded CF calendars without closing caller-owned datasets.
- Enforce input units across segments and saved artifact identity.
- Fit training means from simulations, independent of window counts.
- Align convergence references and marker coordinates with the displayed formulas.
- Preserve configuration-only model inspection through shared operator construction.

Full review evidence is retained under
`~/obsidian/reviews/1111-climatology-correctness/`, including the original diff,
expert reports, adversarial review, and follow-up.

## Compatibility and limits

Existing raw data, prepared artifacts, checkpoints, logs, notebook outputs,
third-party pins, and the Isca local patch remain preserved. Raw source hashes
agree between the two isolated smoke runs. Isca and neuraloperator pins remain
unchanged. The initial smoke artifacts also remain in their separate output directory.

The corrected path requires versioned metadata. Older unversioned prepared data
and checkpoints need fresh preparation and training; they are not silently
reinterpreted under corrected coordinates or normalization.

GPU/MPS execution, full-scale training, new Isca simulations, scheduler execution,
and complete fresh-kernel runs of every historical notebook were not performed.
The effects on full-scale model quality remain unmeasured.
